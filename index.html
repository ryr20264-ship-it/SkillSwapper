ABOUT

A platform built on
genuine exchange

SkillSwapper was born from a simple observation: everyone has something valuable to teach, and everyone has something they want to learn. Yet we spend thousands on courses when the expertise we need might be just one conversation away.

We built a platform that turns knowledge into currency — not dollars, but reciprocal human investment. Teach what you know. Learn what you don't. Grow in ways no course can replicate.

OUR STORY

Why we built SkillSwapper

❝

We were two friends with complementary skills — one a designer who couldn't write code, the other a developer who couldn't design. By learning from each other, we discovered that valuable knowledge already exists within every community. We believed there should be a trusted space where people could connect, exchange skills, and grow together through collaboration.

So we built one. SkillSwapper is our bet that the future of learning is human — peer-to-peer, reciprocal, and rooted in genuine community.

Last updated: July 27, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit SkillSwapper. They help us recognize your browser, remember your preferences, and improve your overall experience on the platform.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Required for the platform to function properly. These cookies enable core features like secure login, session management, and account authentication. They cannot be disabled.

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how users interact with SkillSwapper, including pages visited, time spent, and navigation patterns. This data is anonymized and used solely to improve the platform experience.

Preference Cookies

Remember your settings and choices, such as language preferences, theme selection (dark/light mode), and notification preferences, so you don't have to set them every time you visit.
